# Per-tenant depth may be passed by at most one sampling interval of the tenant's own creates

A guardrail on what tests may assert. With sampled per-tenant counts, creates inside one sampling interval are checked against the last sample, so a burst can pass the limit; at 1,000 creates/s and 5 s, up to 5,000 over a 100,000 limit (Proposals › Limits and counters). Once a sample shows the tenant at or above its limit, every further admission is refused until the count drops.

## Setup
- Engine config: `tenant_max_queue_depth: 3` for **reports** (override), `queue_depth.sample_secs: 5`.
- `{tenant_a}` has 2 `queued` tasks in **reports**; the latest sample (taken at `T0`) shows 2. No worker claims.

## Exchange 1 — burst of four creates inside one interval (`T0+0.1s` … `T0+0.4s`)
Four `POST /tasks` (**render**, **reports**) by `{creator_a}`, distinct `Idempotency-Key`s, as in [1.02](1.02-negative-queue-depth-full-by-dimension.md) (row "tenant").
- Each answers `201 Created` or `429` (`queue_depth.tenant`). The first is `201`.
- The test must NOT assert that the second or a later create of the burst is refused.

## Exchange 2 — one more create after the next sample
Sent once `GET /queues/{reports}` (as `{admin}`) shows a `sampled_at` later than the last create of Exchange 1 (G-CLM-13).
- `429 Too Many Requests`, quota subject `queue_depth.tenant`, `Retry-After` in [15, 30] (G-CLM-09, G-CLM-10).

## Side effects
- `{tenant_a}` may hold up to 6 `queued` tasks in **reports** after Exchange 1 (more than the limit of 3).
- After Exchange 2 the count is unchanged.
- metric `te_queue_depth_rejections_total{queue="…reports.render.v1", dimension="tenant"}` increases by at least 1.
