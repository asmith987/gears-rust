# Concurrency slots stay held through the hand-off and are freed only when the task leaves

| | |
|---|---|
| Requirement | `cpt-cf-task-engine-fr-reassignment` (PRD.md:713, "slots … do not change") · DESIGN.md:547-548 · Proposals › Limits and counters › Open questions (other transactions that take slots) |
| Phase | p3 |
| Test level | Integration — slot accounting |
| Gaps | G-CLM-09, G-CLM-10 |

Reassignment and accept do not take or return a slot: the task counts against the queue and tenant limits from its original claim until it leaves `claimed`/`running`. A waiting task of the same tenant cannot slip into the slot during the hand-off.

## Setup
- **reports** with `per_tenant_concurrency_limit: 5`. `{tenant_a}` has 5 `running` tasks, one of them `{task_1}`; `{task_2}` (tenant A) is `queued`.
- `{task_1}` is reassigned at `T0` to (`{worker_a}`, `reports-7f9c-2`).

## Exchange 1 — Another worker claims during the hand-off
```http
POST /api/task_engine/v1/tasks/claim HTTP/1.1
Host: api.example.com
Authorization: Bearer <worker-a-token>
Content-Type: application/json

{ "worker_id": "reports-7f9c-1", "session_id": "{session_1}",
  "queues": ["gts.cf.core.tasks.queue.v1~acme.reports._.render.v1"], "max_tasks": 1, "timeout_secs": 0 }
```
- `429 Too Many Requests`, `resource_exhausted` with `violations[0].subject = "concurrency"` (no `context.reason`), `resource_name` = **reports**, `Retry-After: 1`: `{task_2}` was skipped because tenant A is still at 5.

## Exchange 2 — `reports-7f9c-2` accepts
```http
POST /api/task_engine/v1/tasks/{task_1}/accept HTTP/1.1
Host: api.example.com
Authorization: Bearer <worker-a-token>
Content-Type: application/json

{ "worker_id": "reports-7f9c-2", "session_id": "{session_2}" }
```
- `200 OK` with a new lease, as in [1.02](1.02-positive-only-named-identity-accepts.md).

## Side effects
- queue_tenant(reports, {tenant_a}) used is 5 throughout: unchanged by the reassignment and by the accept.
- When `{task_1}` completes, queue_tenant(reports, {tenant_a}) used changes by -1 and `{task_2}` becomes claimable.

## Notes
- Follows PRD.md:713 ("State, attempt, slots, and deadlines do not change").
