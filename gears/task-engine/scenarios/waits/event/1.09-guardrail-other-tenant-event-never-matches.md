# An event of another tenant never satisfies a tenant's wait

The consumer reads every waitable topic for all tenants, with platform rights. An event **MUST** match only conditions of tasks in the event's own tenant: the candidate probe filters on `owner_tenant_id = event.tenant_id` before anything else. So an `orders.paid` event for order `o-17` in tenant B never resumes tenant A's task waiting on `o-17` (PRD.md:579, DESIGN.md:915).

## Setup
- Event fixtures as in [positive-1.1](1.01-positive-matching-event-resumes-task-with-header-only.md).
- `{task_1}` (tenant A) is `waiting` on condition `{wc_1}`: `orders.paid`, subject type order, `subject_id: "o-17"`, no match expression, unconstrained consume decision (Exchange 1 of positive-1.1).
- Tenant B also has an order whose ID is `o-17` (subject IDs are not unique across tenants).

## Exchange 1 — `{bev_1}`: orders.paid, `tenant_id {tenant_b}`, subject `o-17`, at T0+1m
Delivered on the engine's static subscription (DESIGN.md:911).

## Expected outcome
- The probe `(event_type, owner_tenant_id = {tenant_b}, event_subject_id = o-17)` finds no candidate for `{task_1}`; the handler returns `Success` and the offset advances (DESIGN.md:926).
- `{wc_1}` stays `pending`; `{task_1}` stays `waiting`.

## Exchange 2 — `{bev_2}`: orders.paid, `tenant_id {tenant_a}`, subject `o-17`, at T0+2m
- `{wc_1}` is `satisfied`; `{task_1}` moves to `queued`; the stored header is that of `{bev_2}`.

## Side effects
- After Exchange 1: no `te_event_inbox({bev_1})` row in tenant A's domain; history of `{task_1}` gains nothing.
- After Exchange 2: history of `{task_1}` gains `waitConditionSatisfied` and `taskResumed`.

## Notes
- This holds even for a tenant **subtree**: an event of `{tenant_a1}` (child of A) does not match a wait of a task owned by `{tenant_a}`; the match is on the exact owner tenant (DESIGN.md:915, :830).
- Until #5493 (DESIGN.md:912), tenant events do not reach the engine at all; the scenario asserts the specified behaviour.
