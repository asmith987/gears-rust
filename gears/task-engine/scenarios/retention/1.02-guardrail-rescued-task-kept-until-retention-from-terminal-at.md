# A task that is live when its slot is recycled is rescued, and kept for its full retention from `terminal_at`

| | |
|---|---|
| Requirement | `cpt-cf-task-engine-fr-retention` (PRD.md:1091, :1092) · ADR-0009:44, :45, :62, :88, :115, :118 · DESIGN.md:847, :854-856, :859 |
| Phase | p1 |
| Test level | Integration — days of clock + cleanup jobs |
| Gaps | G-OPS-32 |

Retention counts from the moment the task finished, not from its creation, and never removes an unfinished task. When a creation slot is recycled, the rows of tasks that are still non-terminal are rescued into the `_retained` tables, not truncated. A task created 200 days ago and finished today is therefore kept another 100 days; a task still `scheduled`, `blocked` or `waiting` after the retention period is live work and stays readable with its whole history.

## Setup
- Shipped retention config (`global_retention_days: 100`, `hard_retention_days: 365`).
- `{task_1}` (**render** on **default**, which has no lifetime timeout) was created at `T0-200d` and stayed `scheduled` until it ran; it completed at `T0`.
- When its creation slot was recycled (about `T0-90d`), it was non-terminal, so its rows were rescued into the `_retained` tables.

## Exchange 1 — At `T0+99d`
```http
GET /api/task_engine/v1/tasks/{task_1} HTTP/1.1
Host: api.example.com
Authorization: Bearer <creator-a-token>
```
- `200 OK`, `state: completed`, `terminal_at: "2026-11-03T10:00:00Z"`, full history.

## Exchange 2 — One maintenance tick after `T0+100d`
- The same request returns `404 Not Found`. Rescued rows are gone within one maintenance tick after `terminal_at + retention` (G-OPS-32).

## Cases

| Case | Differs from the representative | Expected | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| finished after its slot was recycled | (representative) | `200` at `T0+99d`; `404` within one maintenance tick after `T0+100d`; the rescued rows are removed by the bounded delete of expired `_retained` rows (DESIGN.md:859); metric te_retention_removed_total{domain="default", table="te_task_results", how="delete"} increases | PRD.md:1091; ADR-0009:45, :118 |
| still unfinished | `{task_2}` (**render** on **default**) created at `T0-150d` with `run_after = T0+30d`, `scheduled`; its creation slot was recycled at about `T0-40d`; `GET /tasks/{task_2}` at `T0` | `200`, `state: scheduled`, `created_at` 150 days ago; `GET /tasks/{task_2}/events` returns its whole history, including `taskCreated`. metric te_retention_rescued_total{domain="default", table="te_tasks"} increased when the slot was recycled. At `T0+30d` the task becomes `queued` as usual; retention has no effect on its timers | PRD.md:1092; ADR-0009:44, :115 |

## Side effects
- Per row.

## Notes
- The live row is in `te_task_state`, which is never in the ring (ADR-0009:62); only its history rows (input, events, tags) needed the rescue.
- An unfinished task more than `hard_retention_days` after its `created_at` is ended first: [1.03](1.03-positive-hard-retention-limit-force-cancels.md).
